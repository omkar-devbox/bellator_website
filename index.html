Bellator Engineers India Pvt. Ltd. is a specialized Indian engineering company focused on the design and manufacturing of custom-fabricated industrial damper valves for critical process and HVAC applications. With strong expertise in high-temperature applications, large-size fabricated dampers, sealing efficiency optimization, and actuator integration, Bellator delivers engineered solutions tailored to demanding industries including Power, Steel, Cement, Oil & Gas, and Process Plants.

In strategic partnership with Actuatech S.p.A., Italy — a globally recognized manufacturer of pneumatic actuators — Bellator offers integrated damper and actuator solutions designed for reliability, performance, and long-term operational efficiency.

Combining advanced engineering practices, robust manufacturing capabilities, and application-driven design philosophy, Bellator ensures every solution is optimized for real operating conditions, including thermal expansion, continuous duty cycles, and critical shut-off requirements.

Backed by advanced engineering tools such as FEA and CFD analysis, structured quality systems, and compliance-oriented design practices, Bellator continues to serve as a trusted partner for advanced industrial flow control solutions worldwide.

Industries We Serve

Power Generation & Nuclear Facilities

The Operating Reality: Extreme thermal cycles, heavy fly ash entrainment, and strict personnel safety protocols require flue gas isolation where zero downstream leakage is legally guaranteed during live unit maintenance.

The Bellator Solution: Engineered Tandem Louver Dampers and Guillotine Slide Gates fitted with continuous pressurized seal-air cavities to deliver 100% man-safe shutoff under severe positive/negative draft pressures.

Engineered Damper Deployments:
Tandem Louver Damper → Heavy Guillotine Gate → Poppet Isolation Damper →
Operational Environment
Marine Exhaust • EGCS Scrubbers • High Salinity
Compliance: IMO MARPOL Annex VI • Class BV/DNV
Critical Sector // 02 Marine Certified

Marine Vessels & Offshore Platforms

The Operating Reality: Exhaust gas cleaning systems (EGCS) produce highly acidic sulfuric acid condensate (pH < 2) under violent shipboard vibration, demanding corrosion-proof alloys with zero-leakage diverter valves.

The Bellator Solution: Purpose-engineered Marine Butterfly and Diverter Dampers cast and fabricated in Super Duplex and Hastelloy C-276, equipped with marine-certified pneumatic fail-safe actuators and anti-seize high-nickel stems.

Engineered Damper Deployments:
Marine Scrubber Butterfly → 3-Way Scrubber Diverter → Engine Room Fire Damper →
Operational Environment
Hydrocarbon Flare Gas • Cat Cracking FCCU • ATEX
Compliance: ASME B16.34 • API 598 • ATEX Zone 1/2
Critical Sector // 03 ATEX Hazardous Area

Oil, Gas & Petrochemical Refineries

The Operating Reality: Fluid catalytic cracking units (FCCU) and refinery flare headers expose valves to severe differential pressures, explosive gas mixtures, and high thermal transients where unintended leakage triggers catastrophic plant trips.

The Bellator Solution: Double Disc Through-Conduit Gate Valves with mechanical wedge sealing and ATEX-certified Louver Dampers featuring inert gas purge chambers and fire-safe shaft seals.

Engineered Damper Deployments:
Double Disc Gate Valve → Flare Gas Louver Damper → FCCU Severe Service Gate →
Operational Environment
Kiln Preheaters • Clinker Coolers • Smelter Exhaust
Abrasive Loading: > 180 g/Nm³ Dust Entrainment
Critical Sector // 04 Severe Abrasive Service

Cement Plants, Smelters & Heavy Metals

The Operating Reality: Preheater cyclones and clinker cooler lines subject dampers to sand-blast abrasive clinker dust velocity exceeding 25 m/s, causing ordinary valve guides to seize and erode within months.

The Bellator Solution: Self-cleaning Guillotine Slide Gates fitted with external hardened roller tracks away from the gas stream, Hardox 500 replaceable wear plates, and internal castable ceramic refractory insulation.

Engineered Damper Deployments:
Severe Guillotine Slide Gate → Refractory Louver Damper → Heavy Counterweight Flap →
Operational Environment
FGD Flue Gas Desulfurization • Wastewater Scrubbers
Fugitive Emissions: TA-Luft • EPA Method 21 Clean Air
Critical Sector // 05 Zero Fugitive Emissions

Environmental, Chemical & Process Utilities

The Operating Reality: Wet FGD scrubbers, wastewater aeration, and sugar distillery bio-gas carry aggressive halogen vapors and hydrogen sulfide (H2S), requiring absolute vapor containment and zero fugitive shaft leakage.

The Bellator Solution: High-integrity Butterfly and Multi-Louver Dampers with spring-loaded live PTFE packing, fluoropolymer resilient elastomer blade seals, and 316L/Hastelloy bodies engineered for decades of maintenance-free isolation.

Engineered Damper Deployments:
FGD Scrubber Damper → Butterfly Control Damper → Aeration Louver Valve →
