Butterfly Damper Valves

Precision engineered single flap butterfly damper for harsh flue gas isolation and aerodynamic throttling.

Detailed Engineering Overview

Manufactured in sizes up to 7,000 mm in round shape and up to 12,000 mm in rectangular shape. Features concentric or offset single disc for corrosive, abrasive, and hazardous gas flows.

Key Performance Highlights

  • ✓ Standard sizes up to Ø7,000 mm round & 12,000 mm rectangular
  • ✓ Engineered for low pressure drop and aerodynamic blade profiles
