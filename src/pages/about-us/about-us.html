Failure Prevention System

Design Failure Modes & Effect Analysis (DFMEA)

At Bellator Engineers, we prioritize proactive measures to prevent failures and ensure reliability. We conduct Design Failure Modes and Effect Analysis (DFMEA) for every product design, following international standards.

This structured approach enables us to identify and address potential problems or failures and their resulting effects on the system or process before any adverse events occur in the field.

01

Potential Failure Mode Identification

Evaluating blade bending moments under peak differential pressure, shaft seizure at 1200°C, and abrasive particle entrapment in sealing lands.

02

Root Cause & Severity Classification

Determining Risk Priority Numbers (RPN) for thermal stress differentials, gland leakage, actuator stall torque, and aggressive flue-gas acid condensation.

03

Proactive Design Countermeasures

Incorporating external self-aligning bearings, lantern ring purge seals, refractory anchors, and mechanical stops before cutting metal.
